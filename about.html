ABOUT ABC TUTORING

Personal tutoring with simple online scheduling

ABC Tutoring is a small K–12 tutoring service, with much of our work focused on middle school students. Families can choose from in-person and online sessions.

OUR SERVICE

One student, one tutor, one focused hour

Sessions are generally one-on-one and usually last 60 minutes. We offer elementary math through Algebra II, science, and elementary reading.

Because availability changes from week to week, the website is designed to show each tutor’s current openings and individual hourly rate. Parents can select an available time and book online without needing to call or text first.

Frequently asked questions

What grades do you tutor?

ABC Tutoring works with K–12 students, with a strong focus on middle school students.

What subjects do you offer?

Elementary math through Algebra II, science, and elementary reading.

How long is a session?

Sessions are usually one hour and generally one student at a time.

How much does tutoring cost?

Rates range from $40 to $55 per hour depending on the tutor. Each tutor profile shows that tutor’s individual rate.

How does booking work?

Choose a tutor, review their current weekly openings, select a time, and enter the requested parent and student information online.

Do you offer in-person and online tutoring?

Yes. ABC Tutoring offers both in-person and online sessions.
